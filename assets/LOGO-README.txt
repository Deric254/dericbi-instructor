LOGO INSTRUCTIONS
=================

Place your logo file here as:
    assets/logo.png

Recommended: Square PNG, 256x256 or 512x512 pixels, transparent background

The app will automatically load logo.png.
If logo.png is not found, the app uses the built-in bar chart icon.

For the Windows installer icon:
    assets/icon.ico  (256x256, ICO format)

You can convert PNG to ICO at: https://convertio.co/png-ico/
